Inside this study

Gold tissue has a shorter refractory period than blue tissue. Conduction speed is the same on both paths. “Find a rhythm” delivers a second impulse while the blue entrance is still refractory. Its wave stops there, while the other wave travels around the ring and returns after the blue tissue has recovered.

The impulse opens the filaments as it passes. Their gathering follows an interpolation of the neighboring sites’ refractory periods. Between-site geometry is an artistic representation, not a continuous tissue model. Every traveling impulse and block comes from the Counterpoint simulation.
