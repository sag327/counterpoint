# Echo

## The musical idea

Notes that first follow one another then gather almost into a single sound. A small extra note sometimes returns afterwards, like a call answered.

## What the tissue is doing

Two routes run through the junction between atria and ventricles. A premature beat can find one route closed and travel slowly down the other, then return by the first.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, silent nodal sites form fast and slow pathways with their own refractory curves and decrement. The node is a short chain, not a continuum.

## A note from history

Denes and colleagues (1973) demonstrated dual AV-nodal pathways in patients with paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia.

## Try this

Tap one early beat and listen for the sound that comes back.
