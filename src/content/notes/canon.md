# Canon

## The musical idea

One melody sung by two voices, the second following the first at exactly half a lap. Close together, the second voice is lost; well spaced, it settles in.

## What the tissue is doing

A single wave circles a ring. A well-timed second wave can follow it through the gap, and the two spread to an even spacing.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, an eight-site ring has restitution, entry slowing and one link that conducts one way. Real rings are continuous tissue with curved wavefronts.

## A note from history

Mines (1913, 1914) described circulating excitation in heart muscle and its possible relation to tachycardia.

## Try this

Add one well-timed tap and watch the second voice find its place.
