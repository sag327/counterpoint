# Prelude

## The musical idea

A held silence, then a slow return to pace. The beats quicken a little at a time until the old tempo settles again. If you wish, a gentle sway follows the breath.

## What the tissue is doing

After a spell of fast pacing, the natural pacemaker is slow to restart. It then recovers over several beats back to its resting rate.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, one sinus focus has overdrive suppression, and an optional breath term varies the cycle. Ion channels and autonomic reflexes are not modelled.

## A note from history

Glass and Mackey, From Clocks to Chaos (1988), treat biological rhythms as dynamical systems.

## Try this

Hold a tempo for a little while, then let go and listen for the return.
