# Hemiola

## The musical idea

Two steady tempos, at first unrelated, slowly learn to count together: three beats of one for two of the other. Listen closely and they lock.

## What the tissue is doing

Two independent pacemakers each nudge the other's timing when they fire. With enough influence their rhythms settle into a fixed ratio.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, one focus is protected and a coupling entry shifts its next firing by a sinusoidal curve. Cell-level coupling is reduced to one number.

## A note from history

Moe, Jalife, Mueller and Moe (1977) built a mathematical model of parasystole. Reich's Piano Phase (1967) offers a musical touchstone.

## Try this

Turn up Listen to each other slowly and notice when the two voices settle.
