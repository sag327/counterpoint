# Meter

## The musical idea

Beats arrange themselves into groups, such as five against four or three against two. No one wrote the groups in; they appear as the pace quickens.

## What the tissue is doing

As the heart is paced faster, each beat takes a little longer to cross the junction. Eventually one beat fails to cross, and the cycle begins again.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, a chain of three silent nodal sites with restitution and decrement produces the grouping. Real nodal tissue is not a three-site chain.

## A note from history

Guevara, Glass and Shrier (1981) showed phase locking and irregular dynamics in periodically stimulated cardiac cells.

## Try this

Hold a tempo and quicken it slowly until a beat goes missing.
