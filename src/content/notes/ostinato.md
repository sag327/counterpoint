# Ostinato

## The musical idea

A quick figure repeats without end while a slower voice shadows it, playing only some of its notes. Change the conditions and the shadow changes with it.

## What the tissue is doing

A fast circuit in the atria fires far more often than the ventricles can follow. The junction passes only some beats, in a pattern that shifts.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, a ten-site atrial ring feeds a nodal chain, then the His-Purkinje and ventricular sites. The ring is a graph, not continuous tissue.

## A note from history

Waldo and colleagues (1977) described entrainment and interruption of atrial flutter with atrial pacing.

## Try this

Draw a line across the ring and watch the figure slow, then stop.
