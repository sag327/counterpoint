# Ghost

## The musical idea

One voice falls silent and does not return, even though nothing seems to stop it. A single longer gap brings it back.

## What the tissue is doing

A beat that blocks in one bundle can still reach it silently from the other side. That hidden entry keeps the bundle refractory at the next beat.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, right and left bundle sites have restitution, linked by a transseptal path. Silent penetration is read from the chain of events, not drawn from a separate rule.

## A note from history

Langendorf (1948) described concealed A-V conduction and its effect on later impulses.

## Try this

Hold a tempo, then leave out one beat and listen for the voice to return.
