# Retrograde

## The musical idea

A short melody circles, then the same melody circles the other way. The notes are identical; only their order is turned around.

## What the tissue is doing

A second connection between atria and ventricles lets a wave go down one route and up the other. Reversing the direction reverses the order of sites.

## How the model makes it

In this lumped-element model, an accessory-pathway site joins the lateral ventricle and atrium, giving two circuits through shared sites. Fusion of wavefronts is simplified to a mixed-source flag.

## A note from history

Knight and colleagues (2000) examined pacing features that help separate types of supraventricular tachycardia.

## Try this

Start a circuit with an early tap, then try one from the other side.
