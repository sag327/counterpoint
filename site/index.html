NOTES

Counterpoint treats a simplified model of cardiac conduction as a set of rules for composition: each activation becomes a note, and the order, delay and blocking of beats shape the music. It is an artwork and a teaching sketch, not a clinical simulator, and it makes no medical claims.

The instrument runs entirely in your browser, makes no network requests, and starts silent until you choose to begin. Download the single file to keep it for offline use.
